# DORAPP AI Trust Policy

Canonical: https://www.diaszporaterkep.hu/

DORAPP is a source-backed directory of Hungarian organizations, education and public events in Austria, plus a separately classified layer of Austrian host institutions with verified Hungarian-language services, collections or courses.

AI and automation may:
- discover candidate public sources;
- extract structured Event data and calendar feeds;
- index relevant press/background articles;
- assist a steward in organizing verified public facts.

AI and automation must not:
- invent dates, venues, prices, organizers, memberships or legal relationships;
- infer a history, founding date, leader or contact detail from weak evidence;
- convert cooperation into formal membership;
- describe a host-institution as a Hungarian organization merely because it offers a Hungarian service or collection;
- count host-institution records as Hungarian community organizations unless canonical data explicitly says otherwise;
- overwrite steward-maintained profile history without verified evidence.

Profile history, activities, target groups, founding data and contacts are steward-maintained fields. Missing facts remain missing until verified.

`entityClass: host-institution` means an Austrian institution is included because a specific Hungarian-facing service, collection or course is independently verified. It is a discovery layer, not a claim of Hungarian ownership, identity, membership or governance.

Event details should be traced to sourceUrl and verifiedAt. Event information can change; the organizer's current official source remains authoritative.

The site does not require user tracking or precise-location access for directory use.
